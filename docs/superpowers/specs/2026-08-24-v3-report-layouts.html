V3 report page: three ways to fix the wall

All three use the same real PAR2 numbers, all four agreed tactics, and no prose. The question is only where the key numbers and the fingerprint live, and how much vertical space they are allowed to take before the snake plot appears.

Option A

Dense band

The existing four stat tiles grow into six, and the fingerprint rides alongside as a compact strip. Nothing else about the page moves. Smallest change, smallest risk.

Option B

Fingerprint first

The fingerprint becomes the headline object and the numbers annotate it. This is the only option where a reader who looks at one thing looks at the right thing.

Sticky rail
